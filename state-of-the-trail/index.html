Notes from the road

01 / THE ROUTE

Every border is a decision.

Only neighboring states can connect. You can head north, south, or east to find a way west. Click an earlier stop to retrace your route.

02 / THE STAKES

Real proposals. Fictional fallout.

Bad encounters draw from the supplied legislation spreadsheet. Its summaries and categories shape the story; health losses and costs are invented game mechanics, not claims about a bill’s legal effects.

03 / YOUR PEOPLE

Five travelers. No extra lives.

Use money to soften a setback or take the health loss. Community support can help you recover. Travelers who reach zero health die; get at least one person to Oregon.
